Who we help

Hearing Services Program and private services for adults. Tell us how your care is funded when you book and we will take it from there.

Self-funded retirees

Hearing products and services for retirement, for all levels of hearing loss. Stay connected with the people you love.

Pensioners and veterans

Help to access suitable hearing solutions, with a focus on subsidised assistance through the Hearing Services Program.

Workers compensation

As a registered provider with the NSW State Insurance Regulatory Authority (SIRA), we offer rehabilitation services and hearing aids for workplace noise-induced hearing loss.

NDIS participants

We help NDIS participants use their supports for their hearing, as an accredited Hearing Services Program provider.
